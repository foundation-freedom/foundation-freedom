Purpose

The Paramotor Freedom Network prototypes decentralized aerial support for emergency logistics, aerial mapping, and rapid communication. By forming voluntary mutual-aid chapters, the network showcases how aligned incentives and transparent governance outperform centralized command structures.

Budget & Funding Goal

Initial outfitting for five volunteer pilots, training materials, and safety infrastructure: $45,000.

How to Support

  • Contribute to the equipment fund for gliders, motors, and safety gear.
  • Offer training venues or specialized instruction.
  • Share contacts within communities that can benefit from rapid-response flight support.

Status

Recruiting core pilots and building chapter governance protocols. Flight testing begins Q3 with transparency reports issued monthly.
